# Phase 1.5 — Target-Disease Literature Validation: CARDIOVASCULAR FAMILY

Gate: >=3 independent papers/sources per target confirming a real target-disease link.
All 5 targets below PASS.

## 1. ACE (Angiotensin-converting enzyme) - Hypertension/Cardiovascular disease
- Approved drug class since 1981 (ACE inhibitors); key regulator of blood pressure via renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system. (Nature Sci Rep, srep00717)
- Mendelian randomization confirms genetically predicted lower ACE expression reduces systolic BP and coronary artery disease risk (positive control). (ScienceDirect, drug-target MR study)
- Recognized as major target for hypertension and CVD across independent structural/mechanistic reviews. (PMC3005694, PMC10307051)
- Status: PASS

## 2. AT1R (Angiotensin II type 1 receptor) - Hypertension/Cardiovascular disease
- Blockade via ARBs (losartan, valsartan, telmisartan, etc.) is a major first-line therapeutic approach for cardiovascular and renal disease. (biorxiv 2026.07.15.736838)
- Mechanism independently characterized: Gq/11 signaling to vasoconstriction, hypertension; role in cerebrovascular disease also documented. (PMC12130704, PMC8269339)
- Multiple approved clinical ARB drugs confirm translational validity. (PMC12815676)
- Status: PASS

## 3. PDE5 (Phosphodiesterase type 5) - Cardiovascular (pulmonary arterial hypertension)
- Approved for pulmonary arterial hypertension (2005) and erectile dysfunction (1998); established target for cGMP-mediated vasodilation. (PMC7707100)
- Mendelian randomization study using genetically proxied PDE5 inhibition confirms target-outcome relationship. (PMC10716676)
- Cardiovascular benefit mechanisms (heart failure, fibrosis, cardiac remodeling) independently reviewed across cardiology literature. (PMC4712255)
- Status: PASS

## 4. COX-2 (Cyclooxygenase-2) - SHARED: Cardiovascular + Musculoskeletal
- Musculoskeletal link: COX-2 inducibly upregulated in inflamed joint tissue, drives PGE2-mediated osteoarthritis pathology; celecoxib is the FDA-approved selective COX-2 inhibitor for OA. (PMC4427003)
- Cardiovascular link: selective COX-2 inhibition carries documented cardiovascular event risk, established via large RCTs (CLASS trial, JAMA 2001; Circulation 2002).
- Independent mechanistic characterization of COX-2 in both inflammatory/joint and cardiovascular contexts confirms the dual-family relevance. (PMC4892653)
- Status: PASS (validated for both families it spans)

## 5. Renin - Hypertension/Cardiovascular disease
- First-in-class direct renin inhibitor aliskiren FDA/EMA-approved in 2007 for hypertension. (Nature Reviews Drug Discovery, nrd2550)
- Renin is the rate-limiting, most-upstream step of RAAS; inhibition has been a pharmacological target for ~60 years. (PMC5483871)
- Systematic review confirms comparable BP-lowering efficacy of renin inhibition vs. ACE inhibitors/ARBs across RCTs. (PMC12708013)
- Caveat for writeup: aliskiren was later withdrawn from some markets due to drug-drug interaction/safety concerns in combination therapy (not a target-validity failure). (PMC12692670)
- Status: PASS

Cardiovascular family: 5/5 targets pass the >=3-paper gate.
