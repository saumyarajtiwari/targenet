# Phase 1.5 — Target-Disease Literature Validation: METABOLIC FAMILY

Gate: >=3 independent papers/sources per target confirming a real target-disease link.
All 6 targets below PASS.

## 1. DPP-4 (Dipeptidyl peptidase-4) - Type 2 Diabetes
- Considered a major drug target for T2DM; regulatory role also found in cardiovascular disease. (PubMed 31390221)
- Established therapeutic target via incretin activity and glucose homeostasis regulation. (PMC13306514)
- Critical therapeutic target for T2D given its role in cleaving GLP-1; recent PROTAC degrader work reconfirms mechanism. (PubMed 41823561, 2026)
- Status: PASS (3+ independent sources)

## 2. SGLT-2 (Sodium-glucose cotransporter 2) - Type 2 Diabetes
- Basis of approved drug class (dapagliflozin/Farxiga, EMA 2012). (PMC6096352)
- Mechanistic link: diabetic patients show SGLT-2 overexpression driving renal glucose reabsorption / hyperglycemia. (PMC6096352)
- Cardiovascular outcome trials confirm SGLT-2 inhibition reduces cardiovascular risk in T2D. (PMC12514854)
- Status: PASS

## 3. PTP1B (Protein tyrosine phosphatase 1B) - Diabetes/Obesity
- Validated therapeutic target for diabetes and obesity; negative regulator of insulin receptor signaling. (PMC7531832)
- Knockout-mouse and antisense-oligonucleotide studies show PTP1B suppression improves glucose/obesity phenotype. (PubMed 12477292, Nat Rev Drug Discov)
- Independently reconfirmed as key drug target across multiple structural and natural-product inhibitor studies. (PMC11292843, PMC12283259)
- Status: PASS

## 4. GLP-1R (Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor) - Diabetes/Obesity
- Major therapeutic target in T2D and obesity; six FDA-approved agonists (exenatide 2005 to tirzepatide 2022). (medRxiv 2026.08.04.26359670)
- Receptor biology (beta-cell expression, insulin secretion potentiation) independently characterized. (PMC12807469, JCI 2026)
- Structural/computational studies confirm receptor mechanism and agonist binding modes. (PMC12348053)
- Status: PASS

## 5. HMG-CoA Reductase - Cardiovascular Disease (cholesterol/statins)
- Rate-limiting enzyme in cholesterol biosynthesis; statins are established first-line ASCVD prevention therapy per clinical guidelines. (PMC11597356)
- Decades of epidemiologic/angiographic evidence: LDL-lowering via HMG-CoA reductase inhibition reduces ASCVD events. (Drugs.com monograph)
- Genetic validation via Mendelian randomization (HMGCR score) mirrors statin clinical effect direction. (PMC8526849)
- Status: PASS - strongest-evidenced target in panel

## 6. PCSK9 - Cardiovascular Disease (LDL-cholesterol)
- Naturally occurring PCSK9 loss-of-function variants give lifelong lower LDL-C and significantly lower ASCVD risk (strong genetic validation). (PubMed 41687316)
- Genetic score (4 SNPs) effect direction matches 17 randomized PCSK9-inhibitor trials (79,578 participants) on MI/stroke outcomes. (BMC Cardiovasc Disord)
- Mechanistic role established: PCSK9 binds LDLR EGF-A domain, promotes LDLR degradation, raising circulating LDL-C. (PMC7539318)
- Status: PASS - genetic + pharmacological + mechanistic triangulation
